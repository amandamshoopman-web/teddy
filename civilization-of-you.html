📖 How to Play
The Ages
There are 8 Ages — from the Settlement to the Legacy. You advance by doing, not by reading. Every quest completed and every deed logged earns Influence Points that build your civilization. Each Age unlocks stronger buildings, new map districts, and new abilities to push back your rival.
The Rival
Your rival civilization does not wait for you. They expand on their own schedule toward January. The more you log and complete, the more territory you claim. January is the final turn — whoever holds more of the realm wins the campaign.
The Daily Loop
Each day you log in, the Advisor issues a quest. Open the Journal to respond. You can also log any real-world deed at any time — a networking email sent, a resume updated, a hard conversation had. Anything counts. It all builds your empire.
The Win Condition
January is not just a job deadline. It's the end of a campaign where you figured out who you are and what you want. Your win condition is whatever you defined it to be. The game exists to get you there.
